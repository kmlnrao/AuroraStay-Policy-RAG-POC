AuroraStay Hotels is a mid-sized hotel group operating 18 business and leisure properties across major cities in the USA. The group adds 3 to 4 new properties every year.

Hotel staff handle around 1,200 guest queries per week related to:

Room types

Check-in/check-out

Cancellation policies

Refunds

Breakfast

Parking

Pets

Amenities

Additional charges

Simple queries can be answered using existing FAQs. However, around 40% of queries are complex and require information from multiple documents, such as:

Booking terms

Cancellation policies

Room information

Guest guidelines

Manually combining this information takes 8 to 12 minutes per query and results in a 15% to 20% error rate, as answers may be incomplete or incorrect.

As the hotel group grows, the number of properties and property-specific policies also increases. With 45% annual staff turnover, institutional knowledge is constantly lost.

Objective

The objective is to build a POC for a user interface for AuroraStay Hotels where hotel staff can enter natural-language questions and receive answers based on the hotel's documents.

The application should:

Provide a simple UI where staff can enter their questions.

Retrieve the most relevant information from the hotel's document collection.

Generate an answer grounded only in the retrieved information.

Display the supporting source information along with the answer.

Reduce the number of queries that require manual lookup or escalation, rather than focusing on reducing the time spent handling each query.

Since this is a POC, the application will initially be internal-facing. This will allow the hotel team to test it extensively, ensure that user queries are answered consistently, and validate the accuracy and reliability of the system before deploying it for broader use.

Note: At this stage, the objective is not to reduce query-handling time. The primary objective is to determine whether the system can reliably answer a meaningful proportion of queries without requiring manual effort.

Policy Documents
aurorastay_booking_terms_and_cancellation_policy.pdf (5 pages)
Covers individual transient reservation terms, including Flexible, Advance Purchase, member/promotional, and group rate plan rules; cancellation deadlines; no-show charges; modification procedures; taxes and deposit handling; and a six-property quick-reference matrix.

aurorastay_room_and_property_information.pdf (5 pages)
Details room types and occupancy limits, including King, Double Queen, Accessible King, and Suite rooms; check-in/check-out times; early arrival and late departure handling; guest amenities such as breakfast, Wi-Fi, fitness facilities, luggage storage, and cribs; parking and pet policies; service animal procedures; and accessibility request guidelines.

aurorastay_refunds_and_additional_charges_guide.pdf (4 pages)
Outlines refund eligibility for Flexible, Advance Purchase, duplicate, and service-recovery scenarios; itemized additional charges such as parking, pet fees, late checkout, smoking remediation, damage, and lost keys; incidentals authorization and release procedures; and the five-step refund dispute workflow.

aurorastay_guest_conduct_accessibility_and_resolution_guidelines.pdf (5 pages)
Defines guest conduct rules covering quiet hours, smoking, threats, illegal activity, and visitors; ADA accessibility and service animal handling; safety and privacy protocols; the five-stage complaint resolution process; and the escalation matrix for danger, discrimination, privacy, charge disputes, and legal or media requests.

Evaluation and Test Data Files
evaluation.csv (10 queries)
Contains benchmark evaluation queries with expected answers and relevant policy contexts covering check-in times, cancellation rules, pet fees, smoking charges, service animals, refunds, and accessibility procedures.

test.xlsx (10 queries)
Contains test queries with expected answers and relevant contexts for evaluating the pipeline across booking, property information, guest conduct, and refund policy scenarios.
